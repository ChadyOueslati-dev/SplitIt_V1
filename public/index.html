SplitIt keeps the running tab for a flat, a trip, or a project team. It nets every expense down to a single position per person, then works out the shortest set of transfers that clears the whole group.

How a group gets settled

Step 01

Start a group

Name it, pick a currency, add the people by email. A flatshare, a road trip, a club committee.

Step 02

Log what you spend

Who paid, how much, who it covers. Split evenly, by exact amounts, or by weighted shares.

Step 03

Read the balance

Every member gets one number: what the group owes them, or what they owe the group.

Step 04

Pay it off

SplitIt names the exact transfers. Run them through the sandbox gateway and the ledger updates.

Why the transfer count drops

A group of six people who each owe each other something can end up with fifteen separate IOUs. SplitIt does not track pairs. It tracks one net position per person, then matches the biggest debtor to the biggest creditor until everyone sits at zero.

That takes at most five payments for six people, instead of fifteen. The rule for a group of n is n-1 transfers at worst, against n(n-1)/2 pairwise ones.
